超级暗棋 Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This policy applies specifically to “超级暗棋,” developed and published by SunkeeperStudio (the “Game”).

Privacy Policy

1. Data We Collect

The Game does not require an account. SunkeeperStudio does not actively collect or upload your name, email address, telephone number, contacts, precise location, photos, recordings, payment information, or other personal data through the Game.

2. Camera Permission and QR Codes

When you select “Scan Host QR Code,” the Game requests camera permission to read a room QR code displayed on another device. The code contains a local-network connection address. Camera images are processed in real time only on your device and are not photographed, retained, or uploaded by the Game. You may deny or revoke camera permission and enter the host IP address manually instead.

3. Local-Network Multiplayer

When you use Wi-Fi multiplayer, the Game accesses your local network and directly transmits the host's local IP address, connection status, moves, and current game state between two devices on the same network. This data is used to establish and maintain the match and is not sent to servers operated by SunkeeperStudio. The connection uses an ordinary, separately unencrypted WebSocket over the local network; use this feature only on a trusted network and with a player you trust.

4. Local Game Data

In the current version, matches and preferences are primarily processed on the device while the application is running. SunkeeperStudio does not provide cloud accounts, cloud storage, or cross-device synchronization. Clearing application data or uninstalling the Game may delete related data on the device.

5. Third-Party Services

The current version does not integrate third-party advertising, usage analytics, crash reporting, social sign-in, or in-app purchases. The Game uses the system camera and third-party open-source components to scan and display QR codes, but SunkeeperStudio does not receive camera images through them. Your operating system, app store, or network provider may process necessary device and network data under its own policy.

6. Sharing, Sale, and Retention

SunkeeperStudio does not sell or rent your personal data. Local multiplayer data is transmitted temporarily only between devices participating in the match and is processed by the connected players' devices. We do not disclose your data unless required by law, legal process, or a competent authority.

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Parental or guardian guidance is recommended when a child uses the camera or local-network multiplayer features. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, contact us at the address below.

8. Your Rights and Contact

You may deny or revoke camera and local-network permissions, stop a Wi-Fi match, clear local data, or uninstall the Game. For questions about this policy or data security, email sunkeeperstudiolab@gmail.com.
